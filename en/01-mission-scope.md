# 01 · Mission and Scope

## Mission

Greater Türkiye is a community that monitors Türkiye's **external security environment** from public sources. Our goal is to produce **sourced, archived records with an explicit verification grade** on foreign militaries, conflicts in neighbouring regions, defence procurement, exercises, deployments and incidents, and to publish them as open data anyone can use.

Our approach is **outward-looking, analytic and defensive**. We observe, record and put things in context. We do not designate targets, we do not incite, and we do not go into the field.

## Who we are

- A volunteer, zero-budget, community-driven initiative. We have no legal entity.
- **We are not affiliated with any state body, military, intelligence service, political party or company**; we take no instructions or money from anyone and speak for no institution.
- Pseudonymous contributions are welcome. You do not have to reveal your identity.
- Turkish is our primary language; English is the second. Records carry text in both.
- Data and content are CC BY 4.0; code is MIT.

## In scope

| Topic | Examples |
|---|---|
| Foreign military activity | Exercises, deployments, base openings/closures, naval and air activity |
| Incidents | Intercepts, border incidents, attacks, accidents, maritime incidents — with claims attributed |
| Procurement and industry | Arms purchases, contracts, deliveries, export approvals, production capacity |
| Actors | Foreign state bodies, armed forces, non-state armed groups, international missions |
| Sites | Foreign military bases, ports, airfields — from public information |
| Equipment | Platforms and systems (types, variants, inventory claims) |
| Official statements | Ministries, general staffs, international organisations, NOTAM/NAVTEX notices |
| Disinformation | Claims that turn out false are kept on record with status `false` |

## Out of scope

| Topic | Why |
|---|---|
| **Positions and movements of the Turkish Armed Forces, Gendarmerie, Police, MİT and other Turkish security bodies** | Red line. Nothing beyond official disclosures, and those only coarse and delayed. See [02](02-red-lines.md) |
| Turkish domestic politics, elections, parties | Outside the mission |
| Private individuals, personal data | KVKK and ethics; see [03](03-legal-ethics.md) |
| Classified/leaked documents (from any country) | Red line |
| Field collection (photography, reconnaissance, contacting people) | Red line; Law 2565 and equivalents |
| Cyber attacks, unauthorised access, active scanning | Illegal and outside the mission |
| Targeting, operational advice | We are an analytic, defensive community |

## What the data is for

- A sourced, reproducible incident archive for researchers, journalists and analysts.
- A reference against disinformation: who made a claim, when, and whether it was verified.
- Tracking regional trends (exercise frequency, procurement flows, etc.) from public information.

## Principles in brief

1. **Safety first**: when in doubt, don't publish. See [Red Lines](02-red-lines.md).
2. **Attribution**: every contested characterisation is attributed to whoever made it.
3. **Transparency**: every source is archived; every correction is visible in the record.
4. **Human approval**: nothing is published without human approval.
5. **Zero budget**: free tiers only; no payment method is ever registered.
