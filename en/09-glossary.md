# 09 · Glossary

Terms used in records, posts and discussions. Abbreviations are spelled out on first use in text ([08](08-style-guide.md)).

## Intelligence disciplines and method

| English | Turkish | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Open-source intelligence (OSINT) | Açık kaynak istihbaratı | Information collected and analysed from publicly available sources. Our community's only method. |
| Geospatial intelligence (GEOINT) | Coğrafi-uzamsal istihbarat | Analysis of maps, satellite imagery and location data. |
| Imagery intelligence (IMINT) | Görüntü istihbaratı | Intelligence derived from aerial and satellite imagery. |
| Signals intelligence (SIGINT) | Sinyal istihbaratı | Interception of electronic signals and communications. A state activity; **we don't do it.** |
| Electronic intelligence (ELINT) | Elektronik istihbarat | A branch of SIGINT: intelligence derived from non-communications electronic emissions such as radar. A state activity; **we don't do it.** |
| Communications intelligence (COMINT) | Haberleşme istihbaratı | A branch of SIGINT: intelligence derived from the content or traffic of communications. **We don't do it.** |
| Measurement and signature intelligence (MASINT) | Ölçüm ve iz istihbaratı | Technical intelligence derived from the physical signatures of objects and events (acoustic, seismic, spectral, radiation, etc.). A state activity; citing publicly available scientific measurements (e.g. seismic records) is something different. |
| Intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance (ISR) | İstihbarat, gözetleme ve keşif | Umbrella term for information-gathering activities and the platforms that carry them out (reconnaissance aircraft, UAVs, satellites, etc.). |
| Human intelligence (HUMINT) | İnsan istihbaratı | Collecting information from people. **Out of scope** ([02 §3](02-red-lines.md)). |
| Social media intelligence (SOCMINT) | Sosyal medya istihbaratı | Analysis of public social media content. |
| Geolocation | Geolokasyon | Determining where an image was taken ([07](07-geo-time-disputed.md)). |
| Chronolocation | Kronolokasyon | Determining when an image was taken. |
| Admiralty scale | Admiralty ölçeği | Scale for source reliability (A–F) and information credibility (1–6) ([05](05-verification.md)). |
| Independent source | Bağımsız kaynak | A source that obtained the information through a path separate from another source. |
| Circular reporting | Dairesel raporlama | Sources citing each other so that one piece of information appears multi-sourced. |
| Mosaic effect | Mozaik etkisi | Individually harmless pieces of information combining into a sensitive picture. |
| Operational security (OPSEC) | Operasyon güvenliği | Measures to avoid exposing yourself and the community ([04](04-opsec.md)). |
| Disinformation | Dezenformasyon | False information spread deliberately. |
| Misinformation | Mezenformasyon | False information spread without intent. |
| Sock puppet | Kukla hesap | A fake account hiding its owner's identity. **Never** used to interact with targets. |

## Satellites and imagery

| English | Turkish | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Electro-optical / infrared (EO/IR) | Elektro-optik / kızılötesi | Sensors working in visible light (EO) and thermal infrared (IR). EO is limited by cloud and darkness; IR shows differences in heat. |
| Synthetic aperture radar (SAR) | Sentetik açıklıklı radar | A sensor that sends its own radar signal and builds an image from the reflection; unaffected by cloud or night (e.g. Sentinel-1). Its imagery doesn't read like a photograph; interpretation takes experience ([11](11-tools.md)). |
| Ground sample distance (GSD) | Yer örnekleme aralığı | The distance on the ground covered by one pixel of an image (e.g. 10 m). Determines which objects can be distinguished. |
| Revisit time | Tekrar ziyaret süresi | How long it takes a satellite or constellation to image the same point again. Sets the "between two passes" window in chronolocation ([07](07-geo-time-disputed.md)). |

## Aviation and maritime

| English | Turkish | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| ADS-B (Automatic Dependent Surveillance–Broadcast) | ADS-B | System by which aircraft broadcast their position; military aircraft often switch it off. Turkish military aircraft are **not tracked.** |
| AIS (Automatic Identification System) | AIS | Ships' identity and position broadcast; can be switched off or spoofed. Turkish warships are **not tracked.** |
| NOTAM (Notice to Air Missions) | NOTAM | Official notice to aviators (e.g. firing, exercises, closed airspace). |
| NAVTEX (Navigational Telex) | NAVTEX | Broadcast of navigational warnings to mariners (e.g. firing and exercise areas). |
| Flight Information Region (FIR) | Uçuş Bilgi Bölgesi | Airspace in which flight information and alerting services are provided; not a sovereignty zone. Not to be confused with national airspace: a FIR can also cover international airspace. |
| National airspace | Ulusal hava sahası | The airspace above a state's land territory and territorial waters, under its sovereignty. A FIR is a service responsibility, national airspace is a zone of sovereignty; they are separate concepts. The breadth of airspace is disputed in the Aegean; characterisations are attributed ([07](07-geo-time-disputed.md)). |
| Air Defence Identification Zone (ADIZ) | Hava Savunma Tanımlama Bölgesi | Airspace in which a state requests identification. |
| Territorial waters | Karasuları | Belt of sea under the coastal state's sovereignty. Its breadth is disputed in the Aegean. |
| Contiguous zone | Bitişik bölge | A zone beyond territorial waters where the coastal state may exercise limited control to prevent breaches of its customs, fiscal, immigration and sanitary rules. Not a sovereignty zone. |
| Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) | Münhasır Ekonomik Bölge (MEB) | Sea area where the coastal state has sovereign rights over the living and non-living resources of the water column, seabed and subsoil; it must be declared. Not a sovereignty zone. |
| Continental shelf | Kıta sahanlığı | Area of the coastal state's rights over the seabed and subsoil resources. Unlike the EEZ, it doesn't cover the water column and needs no declaration. EEZ and continental shelf delimitation in the Eastern Mediterranean and the Aegean is disputed; the project makes no judgement on it in its own voice ([07](07-geo-time-disputed.md)). |
| Demilitarised status | Askerden arındırılmış statü | Treaty provisions that limit or prohibit military forces, installations or fortifications in an area. The status of the Eastern Aegean islands is disputed between Türkiye and Greece; characterisations on this are attributed ([07](07-geo-time-disputed.md)). |
| Islands, islets and rocks whose sovereignty was not ceded to Greece by treaties (EGAYDAAK) | EGAYDAAK | Türkiye's official term for these geographical features in the Aegean. Greece does not accept this assessment. The project uses the term as its publication language, not as a sovereignty judgement ([07](07-geo-time-disputed.md)). |
| Intercept | Önleme | An aircraft being met by another aircraft for identification/escort. |
| GNSS jamming / spoofing | GNSS karıştırma / aldatma | Suppressing satellite positioning signals / misleading them with fake signals. |

## Platforms and weapons

| English | Turkish | Explanation |
|---|---|---|

| Armed UAV / UCAV | Silahlı İHA (SİHA) | UAV able to carry munitions. |
| MALE (Medium-Altitude Long-Endurance) | MALE | Class of UAV that flies at medium altitude with long endurance. |
| HALE (High-Altitude Long-Endurance) | HALE | Class of UAV that flies at high altitude with long endurance. |
| Loitering munition | Dolanan mühimmat | Single-use munition that loiters over an area and dives onto a target ("kamikaze drone"). |
| Uncrewed surface vessel (USV) | İnsansız deniz aracı (İDA) | Surface vessel without crew. |
| Airborne early warning and control (AEW&C) | Hava erken uyarı ve kontrol uçağı | An aircraft carrying a long-range radar that builds the air picture over a wide area and supports the management of air operations. |
| Ballistic missile | Balistik füze | Missile flying most of its path on a ballistic trajectory. |
| Cruise missile | Seyir füzesi | Guided missile flying within the atmosphere, usually at low altitude. |
| Transporter erector launcher (TEL) | Taşıyıcı-dikleştirici-fırlatıcı | A vehicle that carries a missile, raises it to the firing position and launches it. |
| Surface-to-air missile (SAM) | Karadan havaya füze (KHF) | Air defence missile. |
| Man-portable air-defence system (MANPADS) | Omuzdan atılan hava savunma sistemi | Short-range air defence missile that one person or a small team can carry and fire. |
| Electronic warfare (EW) | Elektronik harp (EH) | Use of the electromagnetic spectrum for attack, protection and support. |
| C4ISR | C4ISR | Command, control, communications, computers, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance systems. |

## Air defence and operational concepts

These terms are here to help you understand wording in sources. In records, the parties' characterisations are attributed ([08](08-style-guide.md)).

| English | Turkish | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Integrated air defence system (IADS) | Entegre hava savunma sistemi | An air defence structure in which radars, air defence missiles, interceptor aircraft and the command-and-control network work as a single whole. |
| Missile engagement zone (MEZ) | Füze angajman bölgesi | A volume of airspace in which responsibility for engagement normally rests with surface-to-air missile systems. Sources sometimes also use it loosely for a system's effective range. |
| Suppression of enemy air defences (SEAD) | Düşman hava savunmasının bastırılması | Operations to neutralise air defence systems temporarily, using electronic warfare or anti-radiation munitions. A doctrinal term; the project never uses "enemy" as a characterisation in its own voice in records. |
| Anti-access / area denial (A2/AD) | Erişim engelleme / alan hâkimiyeti | An approach using long-range missiles, air defence, naval and electronic warfare capabilities to prevent the other side from entering an area (A2) and to restrict its freedom of action within it (AD). |
| Circular error probable (CEP) | Dairesel hata olasılığı | Radius of a circle centred on the aim point within which half of the munitions fired are expected to fall; a measure of accuracy. Manufacturer and government figures are claims and are attributed. |
| Battle damage assessment (BDA) | Muharebe hasar tespiti | Assessment of the damage a strike caused to its target. The parties' damage claims are attributed in `claims[]`; the method of any observation we make ourselves from satellite imagery is written into the record. |
